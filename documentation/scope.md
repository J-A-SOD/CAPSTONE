## PROJECT SUMMARY


This project aims to explore how audio/sound can serve as the primary storytelling/emotional device within an interactive/immersive digital experience. 

Participants will navigate a sequence of audio-visual scenes/environments designed to evoke a variety of distinct emotional atmospheres, eg: stressful, calm, exciting. Participants would actively move through the experience, interacting with certain objects and choosing the pace and time spent in different sections of the project.

A focus of the project would be creating detailed soundscapes and auditory environments that are immersive and responsive to a changing digital space.

The project would be either a VR or Desktop experience likely built in Unity or Unreal, depending on accessibility, software capabilities, time constraints, etc...

The stretch goal is to pair this with live biometric data from a sensor (hear rate, etc...) to understand how certain digital audio environments could influence people's mood/emotions.
